  • Load the sample list, which carries two repeats on purpose, then reverse, sort and remove duplicates.
  • Pass: the sorted output is ascending, the reversed output is the exact mirror, and two duplicates are reported removed.

LL-T5The game, against the legend

  • Play seven people with k of three, then forty-one with k of three, then three people with k of forty-one.

LL-T8The numbering promise holds

  • Read the menu against the enum: the four newest items are fifteen to eighteen.

Requirements LL-R8 LL-R9 Design LL-D9

LL-T9No memory errors on the way through

  • Rebuild with the address sanitizer, then rerun the sessions, the game, and a quit while the list is a circle.
  • Pass: no invalid access and no double free on any run.
  • Not claimed here: leak counting. The sanitizer's leak checker does not run on this platform, so the freeing paths are read rather than measured.
